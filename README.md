# stories

🌌 Maximum Reality Universe

Welcome to the Maximum Reality Universe — a world where perception meets immersion, imagination carries meaning, and stories are alive.

Here you’ll find:
	•	Child-friendly stories starring Mochkil, Azul, Mishmish, Bziz, and more.
	•	Illustrations generated by Gemini using Google Nano Banana Pro prompts.
	•	Open-source storytelling, free for everyone to read, explore, and enjoy.

🐈 Characters & Series
	•	Azul – The Observer. A floating sphynx cat who notices everything. Calm, cosmic, and wise.
	•	Mochkil – CEO Cat Energy. Tuxedo cat, alley-born, full of ideas, Moroccan flair, and unstoppable enthusiasm.
	•	Mishmish & Bziz – Chaos companions. Tiny mouse, grumpy fat cat. Hilarious, slapstick adventures in Moroccan Darija.

Each character has their own series of stories — short chapters, whimsical illustrations, and lessons in curiosity, creativity, and belonging.

⸻

📖 Philosophy

We believe entertainment is a right, not a privilege.

Stories, games, and imagination should be accessible to everyone, regardless of circumstance.
If you enjoy these stories and want to support the author, there’s a Buy Me a Coffee link — contribute any amount you choose, or simply enjoy the universe for free.

Everyone deserves a little magic.
