Stella’s Birthday Splash!

Stella woke up on her very first birthday with a wiggle in her toes and a sparkle in her eyes. A trail of glowing blue droplets shimmered across the floor.

She followed the droplets to a shiny spoon that glowed like sunshine on water. Luna gasped. “You found the Birthday Spoon!”

Stella tapped a tower of colorful cups. DING! A tiny rainbow fountain burst out, sparkling and swirling in the air.

Her walker shimmered and became a Birthday Chariot. Stella zoomed forward, glowing droplets swirling around her wheels.

A friendly dog appeared with a happy wag. WOOF! He trotted ahead, leading Stella like a proud parade guide.

Stella tapped the glowing circle. SPLASH! A magical Birthday Splash Pool appeared, sparkling with rainbow water.

A glowing droplet landed on her spoon. Stella lit up with a warm golden glow. She found her Birthday Magic.

Luna scooped her into a big, splashy hug. “You shine so bright, Stella.” The dog curled beside them.

Stella lifted her spoon and tapped the air. DING. One glowing droplet floated upward like a tiny birthday wish.
