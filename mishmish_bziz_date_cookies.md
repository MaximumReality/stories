---
title: Mishmish Steals the Date Cookies
--- 

*Page 1*  
In a quiet Moroccan kitchen, a tray of warm date cookies cooled on the counter.  
They smelled sweet. They smelled dangerous.

![Date cookies on the counter](mishmish_page1.PNG)

---

*Page 2*  
From behind the teapot, two shiny eyes appeared.  
Mishmish the mouse licked his lips.  
“Shwiya…” he whispered. *(Just a little…)*

![Mishmish peeking at cookies](mishmish_page2.PNG)

---

*Page 3*  
Across the room, Bziz the cat slept in a loaf.  
One green eye opened.  
“Safi.” *(Enough.)*

![Bziz waking up](mishmish_page3.PNG)

---

*Page 4*  
Mishmish leapt.  
Bziz leapt.  
The cookie tray tipped.

“Yallah!” *(Let’s go!)*

![Cookie chaos](mishmish_page4.PNG)

---

*Page 5*  
Cookies flew.  
Mishmish skidded.  
Bziz slipped on powdered sugar and spun like a top.

“Hshouma!” *(Shame!)*

![Slapstick chaos](mishmish_page5.PNG)

---

*Page 6*  
Everything stopped.  
Silence.

Mishmish held one cookie.  
Bziz held… the empty tray.

![The pause](mishmish_page6.PNG)

---

*Page 7*  
Mishmish took a bite.

“Bnin,” he said. *(Delicious.)*

![Mishmish eating cookie](mishmish_page7.PNG)

---

*Page 8*  
Bziz sighed, flopped over, and stole the last cookie instead.  
Mishmish blinked.

“La!” *(No!)*

![Bziz wins](mishmish_page8.PNG)

---

*Page 9*  
They sat together on the floor, crumbs everywhere.  
Sometimes, chaos tastes sweet.

![Crumbs and peace](mishmish_page9.PNG)
